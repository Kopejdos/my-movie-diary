🎬 My Movie Diary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

My Movie Diary is a small, non-commercial hobby project that lets you keep track of the movies you have watched across film collections. This page explains in plain words what data the app stores, why, and who can see it.

Who runs the app

The app is run by Adam Kopecký (Kopejdos), Czech Republic, who is responsible for your data. Contact: mymoviediary.support@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and, if you register with email, your password – stored only as a secure hash, never as the password itself.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, the sign-in service keeps the basic profile Google shares – your email address, Google account ID, name and a link to your profile picture. The app itself only uses the email address to identify your account. We never get access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Profile: the nickname you choose, the date you joined and whether your profile is public.
  • Your diary: the movies you mark as watched, with the date of each viewing, and your watchlist.
  • Sign-in records: the sign-in service keeps technical records of sign-ins (time and IP address) to protect accounts against abuse.

How it is connected

Your nickname, diary and watchlist are linked to your account only through a random account ID. Your email address is used for signing in (also when you sign in with your nickname) and for emails about your account, such as confirming your address. If you sign in with Google using the same email address as an existing account, the sign-in service may link both sign-in methods to that one account.

Who can see it

  • Other users never see your email address or your watchlist. By default your profile is private and nobody else can see your diary.
  • The administrator can see the list of accounts with their nickname, email address, sign-in method, join date and number of logged viewings – only to run and moderate the app.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and the app has no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Public profile (optional)

You can choose to make your profile public on your profile page. A public profile can be opened by anyone with the link (also without an account) and found by nickname in the app's search. It shows your nickname, the month you joined, and the movies you have watched with the date of each viewing, together with the level, achievements and statistics calculated from them. Your email address, watchlist and account details are never shown. You can make your profile private again at any time; it disappears from public view immediately.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland), which also provides the sign-in service. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Movie titles, posters and other movie information come from TMDB; your personal data is never sent to TMDB.

Cookies and local storage

The app stores your sign-in session and a few settings (such as light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we may use it

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (your account and diary) and, for the sign-in records, to keep the app secure. A public profile is shown only because you switched it on.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. When your account is deleted, your profile, diary and watchlist are removed permanently.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected, or have your account and all of its data deleted at any time – just write to mymoviediary.support@gmail.com or open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority (ÚOOÚ).

Age

You must be at least 15 years old to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. See also the Terms of Service.
