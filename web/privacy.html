🎬 My Movie Diary

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

My Movie Diary is a small, non-commercial hobby project that lets you keep track of the movies you have watched across film universes. This page explains what data the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password hash (if you register with email), or your email address from Google (if you sign in with Google).
  • Profile: the nickname you choose.
  • Your diary: the movies you mark as watched, with the date of each viewing, and your watchlist.

When you sign in with Google, the app only receives your basic profile information (email address, name and profile picture) and uses the email address solely to identify your account. We do not access any other Google data.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in and to show you your own progress. Other users cannot see your email address or your diary. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising, and the app has no tracking or analytics.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Movie titles, posters and other movie information come from TMDB; your personal data is never sent to TMDB.

Cookies and local storage

The app stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted at any time by opening an issue in the project's GitHub repository or by contacting the administrator. Your data will be removed permanently.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
